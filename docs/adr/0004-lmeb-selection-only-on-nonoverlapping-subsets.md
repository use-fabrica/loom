# LMEB may drive embedding-model selection only via its non-overlapping subsets

LMEB repackages LoCoMo and LongMemEval as retrieval corpora. Selecting the Engine's embedding model on those subsets and then reporting LoCoMo/LongMemEval End-to-end Benchmark scores would be tuning on our own test set. Therefore embedding-model selection may only use LMEB's non-overlapping subsets — the `[selection]` list in `benchmark/lmeb/subsets.toml` is the authority; the LoCoMo/LongMemEval-derived subsets remain visible in Reports but are diagnostic-only and never a selection criterion. Rejected: treating all of LMEB as diagnostic-only, which would discard its value as a cheap, deterministic selection signal.

Verified 2026-09-01 against the LMEB paper (Zhao et al., "LMEB: Long-horizon Memory Embedding Benchmark," arXiv:2603.12572), the KaLM-Embedding/LMEB repository at commit `a02ae842598183ed162fc90c58ffaae5eec89f12` (`lmeb_benchmark.py`, `src/__init__.py`, `src/tasks/*.py`), and the KaLM-Embedding/LMEB dataset on Hugging Face at revision `f9d4294be4a24b8c16d6bfb59d56a6fec4bd95c0`: LMEB registers exactly 22 MTEB tasks. Two are direct repackagings of our own End-to-end Benchmarks — `LoCoMo` (Maharana et al., ACL 2024) and `LongMemEval` (Wu et al., ICLR 2025) — and stay diagnostic-only. The other twenty are independent datasets from unrelated sources and form the full selection set: `ConvoMem` (Pakhomov et al. 2025), `MemBench` (Tan et al., ACL Findings 2025), `REALTALK` (Lee et al. 2025), `TMD` (Alonso et al. 2024), `EPBench` (Huet et al., ICLR 2025), `KnowMeBench` (Wu et al. 2026), `QASPER` (Dasigi et al., NAACL 2021), `NovelQA` (Wang et al., ICLR 2025), `PeerQA` (Baumgärtner et al., NAACL 2025), `CovidQA` (Möller et al., ACL 2020), `ESGReports` (Macé et al. 2025), `MLDR` (Chen et al. 2024), `LooGLE` (Li et al., ACL 2024), `LMEB_SciFact` (Wadden et al., EMNLP 2020), `Gorilla` (Patil et al., NeurIPS 2024), `ToolBench` (Qin et al., ICLR 2024), `ReMe` (Cao et al. 2025), `Proced_mem_bench` (Kohar and Krishnan 2025), `MemGovern` (Wang et al. 2026), and `DeepPlanning` (Zhang et al. 2026) — replacing the earlier five-dataset guess, which undercounted the safe set rather than overcounting it. `benchmark/lmeb/subsets.toml`'s `[selection]`/`[diagnostic]` tables encode exactly this split; re-verify against the same three sources whenever LMEB's task registry changes.
