# Benchmarks live in this repo; datasets are fetched on demand, never vendored

The four Benchmarks (LoCoMo, LongMemEval, LMEB, BEAM) live in this repo (`benchmark/`, run via `cmd/bench`), not in separate repos. The three End-to-end Benchmarks: their Adapters are the first consumers of the Engine contract and must co-evolve with it, and a separate repo would introduce harness/engine version skew for no benefit. LMEB shares none of that machinery (it drives the standard MTEB harness against candidate embedding models, with no ingestion, answering, or judging) but stays in-repo because it gates this Engine's embedding-model choice and its selection rules are governed here (ADR-0004). Datasets are downloaded and cached at run time, never committed: LoCoMo is CC BY-NC 4.0 and must not be redistributed, and vendoring would bloat the repo. Rejected: one `loom-bench` repo, and one repo per benchmark (the End-to-end three share most of their machinery and differ only in Adapters).
